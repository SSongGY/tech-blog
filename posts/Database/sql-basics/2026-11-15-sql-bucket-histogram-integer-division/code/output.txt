[1-A] BETWEEN 으로 구간을 나누면 (경계값 5 가 두 구간에 걸린다)
  sql> SELECT SUM(temp BETWEEN 0 AND 5) AS b_0_5, SUM(temp BETWEEN 5 AND 10) AS b_5_10, SUM(temp BETWEEN 0 AND 10) AS b_0_10 FROM reading
  b_0_5 | b_5_10 | b_0_10
  4 | 2 | 5
  (1행)

[1-B] CASE 로 반열린 구간 [하한, 상한) 을 쓰면
  sql> SELECT CASE WHEN temp < 0 THEN '영하' WHEN temp < 10 THEN '0~9' WHEN temp < 20 THEN '10~19' ELSE '20 이상' END AS band, COUNT(*) AS n FROM reading GROUP BY band ORDER BY MIN(temp)
  band | n
  영하 | 5
  0~9 | 5
  10~19 | 2
  20 이상 | 3
  (4행)

[2] 정수 나눗셈 버킷 (temp / 5) * 5
  sql> SELECT (temp / ?) * ? AS bucket_start, COUNT(*) AS n, group_concat(temp, ',') AS members FROM reading GROUP BY bucket_start ORDER BY bucket_start
  bucket_start | n | members
  -10 | 1 | -12
  -5 | 2 | -7,-5
  0 | 5 | -3,-1,0,2,4
  5 | 2 | 5,9
  10 | 1 | 13
  15 | 1 | 18
  20 | 2 | 21,22
  30 | 1 | 33

[3-A] 음수에서 / 와 % 가 내는 값
  sql> SELECT temp, temp / 5 AS div, temp % 5 AS rem, (temp / 5) * 5 + temp % 5 AS restored FROM reading WHERE temp < 1 ORDER BY temp
  temp | div | rem | restored
  -12 | -2 | -2 | -12
  -7 | -1 | -2 | -7
  -5 | -1 | 0 | -5
  -3 | 0 | -3 | -3
  -1 | 0 | -1 | -1
  0 | 0 | 0 | 0

[4-A] floor() 로 내림한 버킷

[4-B] floor() 없이 — 나머지가 음수면 한 칸 내린다

[5] 빈 구간까지 0 으로 채우기 (재귀 CTE 로 구간표를 만들고 LEFT JOIN)
  sql> WITH RECURSIVE bucket(start) AS ( SELECT -15 UNION ALL SELECT start + ? FROM bucket WHERE start + ? <= 30 ), tagged AS ( SELECT temp, (temp / ? - (temp % ? < 0)) * ? AS start FROM reading ) SELECT b.start AS bucket_start, b.start + ? - 1 AS bucket_end, COUNT(t.temp) AS n, printf('%.*c', COUNT(t.temp), '#') AS bar_printf, substr('##########', 1, COUNT(t.temp)) AS bar_substr FROM bucket AS b LEFT JOIN tagged AS t ON t.start = b.start GROUP BY b.start ORDER BY b.start
  bucket_start | bucket_end | n | bar_printf | bar_substr
  -15 | -11 | 1 | # | #
  -10 | -6 | 1 | # | #
  -5 | -1 | 3 | ### | ###
  0 | 4 | 3 | ### | ###
  5 | 9 | 2 | ## | ##
  10 | 14 | 1 | # | #
  15 | 19 | 1 | # | #
  20 | 24 | 2 | ## | ##
  25 | 29 | 0 | # | 
  30 | 34 | 1 | # | #
  (10행)

[5-B] printf 의 %.*c 에 반복 횟수 0, 1, 3 을 주면
  sql> SELECT printf('[%.*c]', 0, '#') AS zero, printf('[%.*c]', 1, '#') AS one, printf('[%.*c]', 3, '#') AS three, printf('[%.*c]', -1, '#') AS minus_one
  zero | one | three | minus_one
